⚑ MAHARASHTRA
Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana
Solar agricultural pump support scheme for farmers.

Purpose

Supports farmers in using solar-powered irrigation systems for agricultural activities.

Benefits

Eligible farmers can receive support for solar agricultural pump systems according to applicable Maharashtra Government guidelines.

Eligibility

Eligibility depends on farmer, land and irrigation conditions specified by the applicable scheme guidelines.

▦ CENTRAL GOV
PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi
Income support scheme for eligible landholding farmer families.

Purpose

Provides income support to eligible landholding farmer families.

Benefits

Eligible farmer families receive ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments through direct benefit transfer.

Eligibility

The scheme has eligibility conditions and exclusion categories. Farmer families are identified according to the scheme guidelines.

▦ CENTRAL GOV
Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana
Supports irrigation development and efficient water management in agriculture.

Purpose

The scheme aims to expand irrigation coverage, improve on-farm water-use efficiency and reduce wastage of water.

Benefits

Supports irrigation development, water conservation and efficient irrigation practices.

Eligibility

Eligibility and support depend on the specific component and implementing authority.

▦ CENTRAL GOV
Soil Health Card Scheme
Soil testing and nutrient information for better fertilizer management.

Purpose

Helps farmers understand the nutrient status and health of their agricultural soil.

Benefits

The Soil Health Card provides soil nutrient information and recommendations for fertilizer quantities and required soil amendments.

Information Provided

The card covers parameters such as pH, electrical conductivity, organic carbon and macro and micronutrients.

▦ CENTRAL GOV
MGNREGS – Farm Pond
Supports approved rural employment and water-conservation works.

Purpose

Supports rural livelihood security and approved works related to water conservation and rural asset creation.

Benefits

Eligible rural households can receive wage employment for approved works under MGNREGA.

Eligibility

Eligibility follows the applicable MGNREGA rules and local implementation requirements.
